About me

I am a Geography Undergraduate student at Panskura Banamali College (Autonomous) with a deep interest in understanding the Earth, its physical features, natural processes, human societies, and the complex relationship between people and their environment. My studies in Geography have developed my analytical thinking, observation skills, and interest in areas such as physical geography, human geography, environmental studies, maps, spatial patterns, and sustainable development. I am particularly interested in exploring how geographical knowledge can help us understand real-world environmental and social challenges.

Apart from Geography, I have knowledge of C, C++, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I am interested in programming and web development and enjoy learning new technologies. I aim to combine my geographical knowledge with technical skills to create useful and innovative digital projects.
